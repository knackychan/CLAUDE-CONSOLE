Cells Board

The interface for the harness plan and the brainstorm around it (plans 5, 18, 23, 24 and 25). The compact window keeps what Cells Drawer locked and gains the harness switch, rungs on any CLI, the per-step context view, the usage fills and the replan wait. The Board is the one new page: the window widened to 900 px, the chain of a task on the left as connected cells, one picked thing on the right. Same tokens, same type, same cell motif.

Figures are sample data. Orange still means "needs you" and nothing else; a cut handoff or an estimate is shown by shape (hatched, dashed, a tone) and a word, never by orange. Two zones (Opus review, 2026-10-13): what Fred acts on stays in sight; how, why, history and exact counts sit behind one closed line each. Locked by Fred on 2026-10-13, every decision as proposed except ID-5: a handoff shows no cells for its words.

Board Compact

Every task, step, figure and cost on this page is sample data. Sprites show their still frame.

Design read

Reading this as: the same always-on-top companion, now watching a chain of steps that may run on four CLIs, for one developer who wants to see where the tokens go and which model he is wearing out. Dials unchanged: ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 2.

  • Two new tokens, for the chat only. Orange is "needs you" (a replan to confirm, a question, a model over its pace that the router will leave). Red and green are state (FAIL, done). Blue stays the note being written. A harness has no colour: the rung is words, codex · gpt-6.1-sol · medium. The chat adds --me (violet, Fred's turns) and --ai (the writing blue, the agent's turns), used nowhere else (ID-8).
  • One Doto figure per screen: the task's cost on the Board head, today's spend on the list, the month on Usage.
  • Every quantity is cells, but a handoff's words: context in 10K cells, fills in 5 % cells against an allowance, FAIL rate in four tones. A handoff's word count is a figure in the inspector, never cells (ID-5, Fred: a visual for the words was confusing and cluttered the UI). Unknown is unknown: no cells and the word, never a zero.
  • Motion: the one blinking cell on the live step; the running puzzle and the waiting ? as plan 21 made them. Nothing on the Board moves by itself except the live step.

Two zones (Opus 5.5 review, 2026-10-13)

Fred: the details are good, but they are data for nerds; keep what matters in sight and put the second zone behind a tab or a drawer. The review (design/brainstorm/2026-10-13-interface-review.md) zoned every element; the mock applies its P1 and P2 lists and most of P3.

  • Zone 1, in sight: state (word and sprite), what needs Fred, the one figure that matters on the screen, the next action, the current step. A number stays in sight only if it changes what he does now.
  • Zone 2, one closed line each: how and why. Route notes, exact counts of finished things, provenance (thread ids, paths, diff stats), the checks, logs, scales, config names, and every sentence that explained the design (those moved to titles). The closed line is Label · the one fact worth seeing ▾, muted mono; a FAIL, an open question, a not-found or an over-pace keeps its tone there, and a FAIL or a failing check opens its fold by itself. The app keeps each fold's state per window.
  • One place per number: the handoff words live in the inspector only (the Received and Handoff tabs, the handoff head); the connector chip says handoff and, after a cut, handoff · cut 122 w; the step block lost its in / out line; the inspector head is one line, rung · cost · time.
  • Done things shrink: a finished step is two lines on the chain and on the Task page; a planned step is one; the running task fits the first screen with its FAIL and its live step.
  • Orange is Fred's only: the scout first hint is muted with △, the balance route note is muted (the router already acted), the list's today breakdown is closed and says 3 models over pace · fills only when true.

The chain, read top down

  • Task head: state and age, the title on two lines, step 5c of 8 and the cost; project and effort in its title. Folded tasks are one quiet row whose second line is the orange why of a waiting task, else the step count. Picked: the inspector shows the Now line, prompt.md folded to its Done when, the open questions, the closed ones and the chain log folded.
  • Step block: a finished step is two lines: state sprite and word, number and kind, cost; then the rung as harness · model · effort, the time, and a 15-cell context mini gauge with its figure (or "ctx unknown"). The current, failed, waiting and picked steps keep the full block: the 30-cell context row and the sub line (the FAIL detail whole, the wait, the muted △ scout first hint). Attempt squares show only when a try failed. A planned step is one line.
  • Connector = the handoff: a short line and a chip that names it, handoff; after a cut, handoff · cut 122 w with the cut in ink (ID-1, no orange; ID-5, no cells for words: the counts are the chip's title and the inspector's); dashed as written · not compacted when the brief pass fell back to code; blue dashed writing; a dotted line with no chip before a planned step. Picked: what the next step received, one column, the cut lines struck in place.
  • Fix insert: a bracket under the failed verify holding fix and the second verify, rejoining after. The failed verify keeps its red; the fix shows its escalated rung.
  • Replan: a step of its own kind (Haiku, no tools), orange while it waits for Fred; the inspector shows the new plan as one list with the added steps tagged (a dropped step would be struck in place) and Confirm / Keep old plan / Stop; why it ran and its route fold to one line each.
  • Several tasks: the scope's tasks stack, newest first, every one folded to a quiet row except the picked one. Left / Right moves between them.

The inspector: one picked thing

  • A step: head on one line (state, number and kind; Open chat and Stop at the right) and one meta line, rung · cost · time; context and the words are on the picked block and in the tabs. Four tabs with Chat first (Fred, 2026-10-13): the prompt the service built folds to one line labelled Prompt (not You: Fred did not type it), the agent's turns as bubbles on the left with the blue edge, Fred's own turns on the right with the violet edge, consecutive tool events folded into one row with a failed exit first, the HANDOFF block as a chip with its Next line, the source line at the end. Received (From 02 · 310 w · compacted) and Handoff (For 04 · wrote 412 w · 04 received 290 w; the tab reads 412 → 290 w) show the six headings with Goal, Findings, Open questions and Next open and Files touched, Troubleshooting and the Facts the service added folded to one line each; the lines the brief pass cut stay in place, struck and tagged. Route: the goal, the route notes as chips in the order step_route applied them, the attempts. The tab holds while Up / Down move along the chain.
  • A handoff (connector): one column, what the next step received. The headings with a cut, Open questions and Next are open, the cut lines left in place under their heading, struck and tagged "cut", the label counting "2 kept · 2 cut" (Fred, 2026-10-13: two columns of the same text were unreadable); Goal, Files touched and Troubleshooting fold to one line with "whole". The head counts written, received and cut words. The code checks fold to Checks · 5 of 5 pass · compacted by haiku · $0.004; a failing check shows red there and opens the fold by itself.
  • A task: step 5c of 8 · cost · effort, a Now line (the running step, its rung and time, the next step), the open questions in sight; prompt.md (closed line: its Done when), the closed questions and the chain log (closed line: the last move) fold.
  • Nothing picked (keyboard Esc inside the Board, or a folded task): the inspector shows the measure table of the picked task's project, handoff received against context reached per kind.

Keyboard: Up / Down along the chain (steps and handoffs), Left / Right between tasks, Enter opens the step's session page, Esc clears the pick then leaves the Board. The pick is an ink outline, as a focused control is.

Harnesses in the compact window

  • Settings: the project picker's popover gains a Settings band under Runs off: a card per group, its head a fold line. Harnesses · 2 on · △ cmd not found opens to one row per CLI: a glyph (●, ○, △), the name over its probed version (or cheap models · read and write steps, or the red reason), and one on / off switch that is the state word and the action at once, ... until the service answers; claude reads always on. It opens by itself while a harness is on but not found: that switch turns red and the row says not found · no cmdc.exe on PATH and Set "exe" in config.json, or turn it off. Window runs · auto hand off: off holds the auto hand off switch (ID-2) with its one-line why.
  • Rungs: every model select lists tiers as groups, think / write / read, each holding rungs on any harness that is on and found. OpenCode and Command Code hold cheap models only, listed under read; they are offered on read and write kinds (Fred, 2026-10-14: well-designed code can be written by a cheap model) and never on design, verify or fix, where a pick shows "opencode: not on design, verify or fix steps" and falls back to Auto.
  • Where the rung shows: the task card and the Task page rows (codex · gpt-6.1-sol before the effort), the session head chip, the Board block. A Claude rung keeps today's sonnet · medium.
  • A non-Claude session page: the brief says "context unknown" with no cells and an estimated or unknown spend; tabs Chat and Steps (what the adapter's stream gives), no Agents or Timeline (those come from Claude's hooks); levers Continue and Stop, the rest off with "claude only". A pick whose harness went off at step start shows the route note harness: codex off -> sonnet.

Fills and balancing

  • Usage page, Models block: one row per model (the model, not the harness: Opus through two CLIs is one fill), sorted over pace first, with one bar of 20 cells for the window that binds (day, week or month), the allowance as the full bar, the linear pace as an orange outline cell and the share of pace as the figure (orange over pace: that model is the one the router leaves and the one Fred should rest); the other two windows as text; a click opens the three bars. A model with no allowance says so. The config names fold under the legend.
  • List header: the today strip's breakdown is closed, as locked; under the strip one line, 3 models over pace · fills, shows only while a model is over its pace and opens the breakdown (spend legend, model mix, the over-pace figures and the Usage button). Needs you starts on the first screen.
  • Route notes say what balancing did: balance: sonnet 84 % -> codex · gpt-6.1-sol 31 %, warm: same rung as step 2 (cache), every write rung over pace: least full.
  • Evidence: a Verify FAIL by hour heatmap, folded under Days with its reading as the closed line (opus peaks 14:00 to 17:00); rows per model, 24 columns, four red tones, hover for the count. It is the data for the optional peak-hours table.
