Prompt, Run, Supervise

Plan 48 proposal, explored (not a target): the Board cut to three moments. Type and press Enter; the card shows its scope, its steps and one Start; the supervisor decides between steps; the list gives one status line per task and everything else sits behind opening it.

Tokens, type and cells are the locked Console Cells ones; no new colour. Every task, figure and model below is sample data.

Moment 1: Prompt

Enter sends the prompt; Prep and the planner (plan 47) run with no button and the row says so. When the card is ready its row turns orange with Ready: Start; opening it shows the planner's scope note, the steps and one Start. The enhanced prompt, the route and per-step pickers stay behind Edit steps, never required.

Start posts POST /api/task with supervised: true. A card with no steps (Prep reads it as a chat) starts one session through the existing /api/run.

Moment 2: Run supervised

The first step runs; later steps wait as proposed rows. At each boundary the supervisor (plan 44) wakes once, decides, and in suggest mode leaves one card with Approve and Reject. Approve dispatches the saved decision only.

The one message box is the way to say what comes next: while a step runs it queues guidance for that step (plan 42); at a boundary or on a done task it becomes the supervisor's instruction for the next step.

Moment 3: Supervise

The list has three groups: Needs you, Running, Done. One row per task: mark, title, cost, and one status line (running with its step, needs you with the question or the suggestion, done, failed with why). Steps no longer unfold in the list.

Opening a task shows the step route, the current step's context gauge and the supervisor's last decision. A step row opens its existing inspector (Chat, Received, Handoff, Route, Plan, Files).

What left the screen

  • Continue, Verify, Handoff, Escalate and Retry as buttons: the message box or the supervisor carries them; Retry on a model is in the menu.
  • Add next step and choose next step: the message box; the exact-model form stays in the menu.
  • Hand off task, Finish task, Models and tuning: the task menu.
  • The calendar, the effort slider, Run raw and Review rules: the menu or a key (D4).
