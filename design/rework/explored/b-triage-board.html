Rows never reorder on a single event; they move between bands only when their state changes. Click a running row to open it in place.

Why this shape

The window exists to answer "what do I need to do now?" and "is the rest fine?". So the column is ordered by action: things that wait on you, things running, things waiting for a follow-up, then done. Every row in the top band carries its own fix, so the most common job (answer a question, unblock a denial) takes one click and no navigation.

Window

Frame
Borderless, dark title strip as drag region (high contrast, easy to grab), 8 px corners
Size
400 x 640 default, resizable 360 to 520 wide; height follows the screen
Filter
Project chips with an amber count where something needs you, replacing the select box
Prep
Bottom sheet over the board; the board stays visible behind it

Following progress and cost

  • Sparkline = tool calls per minute over the last 30 min, from steps the service already keeps. Flat line plus "Running" is the Stall? signal you can see before the 10 min rule fires
  • Agent dots: green done, filled blue running, ring waiting. Count of what is real, no guessed percent
  • Opened row: playbook path, spent, context, time, agents, last four steps, levers
  • Budget bar: exact spend (solid) then estimated (light), with a soft daily limit and an amber mark at 80 %. New config: daily_budget_usd; new data: estimated cost for chats
  • Prep shows a typical cost for the chosen route, read from your past runs of that route

Human-proof rules

  • Accordion, not pages: one row open at a time, others dim but stay readable, Esc closes
  • Bands named by action ("Needs you", "Waiting"), states as words in caps with the existing glyphs
  • Figures in Bahnschrift (built into Windows, narrow, very legible digits); IDs and paths in Cascadia Mono
  • Buttons at least 30 px high, verbs only ("Answer", "Open in terminal", "Hand off")
  • Stop confirms in place; nothing uses a dialog; closing the window says runs continue

What it would touch

__main__.py (frameless + minimize and pin through js_api), style.css, app.js (bands, accordion, sparkline as inline SVG, chips, sheet), state.py (per-minute activity buckets, estimated cost, today and week totals). Still no build step, no library.

Trade-offs

  • Uses the full 640 px all the time: the most informative of the three, and the most screen it takes
  • With 8 or more sessions the Running band scrolls; Done stays collapsed to protect space
  • Timeline and Chat still need a full view behind "All steps"
