Cells Board

The interface for the harness plan and the brainstorm around it (plans 5, 18, 23, 24 and 25). The compact window keeps what Cells Drawer locked and gains the harness switch, rungs on any CLI, the per-step context view, the usage fills and the replan wait. The Board is the one new page: the window widened to 900 px, the chain of a task on the left as connected cells, one picked thing on the right. Same tokens, same type, same cell motif.

Figures are sample data. Orange still means "needs you" and nothing else; a cut brief or an estimate is shown by shape (hatched, dashed, a tone) and a word, never by orange.

Board Compact

Every task, step, figure and cost on this page is sample data. Sprites show their still frame.

Design read

Reading this as: the same always-on-top companion, now watching a chain of steps that may run on four CLIs, for one developer who wants to see where the tokens go and which model he is wearing out. Dials unchanged: ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 2.

  • Nothing new in colour. Orange is "needs you" (a replan to confirm, a question, a model over its pace that the router will leave). Red and green are state (FAIL, done). Blue stays the note being written. A harness has no colour: the rung is words, codex · gpt-5.6-codex · medium.
  • One Doto figure per screen: the task's cost on the Board head, today's spend on the list, the month on Usage.
  • Every quantity is cells: context in 10K cells, briefs and notes in cells of 50 words, fills in 5 % cells against an allowance, FAIL rate in four tones. Unknown is unknown: no cells and the word, never a zero.
  • Motion: the one blinking cell on the live step; the running puzzle and the waiting ? as plan 21 made them. Nothing on the Board moves by itself except the live step.

The chain, read top down

  • Task head: state and age, title, project · effort · cost. Picked: the inspector shows prompt.md, the running list of open questions and the chain log.
  • Step block (eight facts, Square's fleet row): state sprite and word, number and kind, the rung as harness · model · effort, cost (~ estimated, ? unknown), then the context row (30 cells of 10K and the figure, or "context unknown"), then brief in and note out as cells of 50 words, and the attempts as small squares (red a failed try, green the one that passed).
  • Connector = the brief: a short line and a chip with the brief's word cells and count. Dashed: the brief fell back to code. A tone step on the cells with "cut 120 w": Findings were cut. Blue dashed: being written. Dotted line: not written yet. Picked: raw note and brief side by side with what was cut.
  • Fix insert: a bracket under the failed verify holding fix and the second verify, rejoining after. The failed verify keeps its red; the fix shows its escalated rung.
  • Replan: a step of its own kind (Haiku, no tools), orange while it waits for Fred; the inspector shows the plan before and after and Confirm / Keep plan / Stop.
  • Several tasks: the scope's tasks stack, newest first, every one folded to a quiet row except the picked one. Left / Right moves between them.

The inspector: one picked thing

  • A step: head (number, kind, rung, cost, time, Open and Stop), Goal, Route notes as chips in the order step_route applied them (rule, pick, demand, floor, hint, cap, budget, balance, harness fallback), What it read (the brief, six headings, folded to its Goal and Next), What it wrote (the HANDOFF block as six blocks; Goal and Troubleshooting carry a green edge: never cut), Facts the service added (git diff --stat between the start and end commits, the failing lines of the last test command), the attempts with their reason.
  • A brief: two columns, raw note and brief, the cut lines struck through on the left, the checks under them in words (length, questions kept, Goal and Troubleshooting whole, continuity with the previous Files touched, no invented path, the Haiku cost).
  • A task: prompt.md, Open questions with the step that opened and the step that closed each one, the chain log.
  • Nothing picked (keyboard Esc inside the Board, or a folded task): the inspector shows the measure table of the picked task's project, brief in against context reached per kind.

Keyboard: Up / Down along the chain (steps and briefs), Left / Right between tasks, Enter opens the step's session page, Esc clears the pick then leaves the Board. The pick is an ink outline, as a focused control is.

Harnesses in the compact window

  • Settings: the project picker's popover gains a Harnesses group under Runs allowed / Runs off: one row per CLI with a glyph and state word (● on, ○ off, △ not found), its probed version, and Turn on / Turn off, pending until the service answers. Claude reads "always on" (Prep and the brief run on it). A row that is on but not found says where to set exe. Below it, a Window runs group with the auto hand off switch (ID-2).
  • Rungs: every model select lists tiers as groups, think / write / read, each holding rungs on any harness that is on and found. OpenCode and Command Code rungs appear under read only; a step row whose kind is a write or think kind does not list them, and a pick that a kind cannot hold shows "opencode: read steps only (cheap tier)" and falls back to Auto.
  • Where the rung shows: the task card and the Task page rows (codex · gpt-5.6-codex before the effort), the session head chip, the Board block. A Claude rung keeps today's sonnet · medium.
  • A non-Claude session page: the brief says "context unknown" with no cells and an estimated or unknown spend; tabs Chat and Steps (what the adapter's stream gives), no Agents or Timeline (those come from Claude's hooks); levers Continue and Stop, the rest off with "claude only". A pick whose harness went off at step start shows the route note harness: codex off -> sonnet.

Fills and balancing

  • Usage page, Models block: one row per model (the model, not the harness: Opus through two CLIs is one fill), three bars day / week / month of 20 cells each, the allowance as the full bar and the linear pace as an orange outline cell. The figure is the share of pace; over pace it is orange, because that model is the one the router leaves and the one Fred should rest. A model with no allowance shows hatched bars and "no allowance".
  • List header: the today strip's breakdown gets the same fills in miniature, one column per model, so "which one am I overusing" is read without leaving the list.
  • Route notes say what balancing did: balance: sonnet 84 % -> codex · gpt-5.6-codex 31 %, warm: same rung as step 2 (cache), every write rung over pace: least full.
  • Evidence: a Verify FAIL rate by hour heatmap, rows per rung, 24 columns, four red tones, hover for the count. It is the data for the optional peak-hours table and the answer to "does the afternoon Opus fail more".

What it would touch

Service: /api/config harnesses and POST /api/harness (plan 5), /api/task with each step's brief and note word counts, start and end commits, the facts block and the chain log (plan 23 / 25), /api/usage with fills per model and the FAIL-rate counts (plan 18), window.json with the Board size.
