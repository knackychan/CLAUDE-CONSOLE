What changes from the locked Board

  • One view. The Board is the window. From 700 px it has two columns; under 700 px the same view is one column and a pick opens the inspector over the chain with ‹ Board to go back. The pill stays the folded form. The list page, the Task page and the Prep page are gone; the session page lives on as a step's Chat tab.
  • Calendar header. It replaces the today strip. The picked day's spend is the one Doto figure, with the spend cells against the limit under it. Two weeks of days run Monday first: tone is the day's spend (the Usage scale), the number is the tasks done that day, today is underlined. A click picks that day: its Done group opens and scrolls into view, and the inspector shows the day. The arrows move a week.
  • Steps are sub-steps. Each step is one plain row on a tree line, indented under its task. There are no blocks or pills. A fix and its re-verify indent one level further under the verify that failed. Step 00 is Prep, in violet like the prompt it came from, and every row is a button.
  • No connector rows. A handoff shows on the step that received it, and only when it has something to say: received cut 122 w, + plan or as written. The whole handoff is the Received tab, where the cut lines are struck in place. The picked-handoff view becomes that tab.
  • Done by day (D8 a). Groups are Needs you, Replied, Working, then Done by local day: today open, older days folded to their label and count. Past 14 days, everything sits under one Older · 23 row that opens in place.
  • Label (D11 a). Every task head carries MMDD-xxxx. A click copies the folder name and the chip says copied for a second; C does the same for the picked task. The task inspector adds Open folder.
  • Select and delete (D10 a). A Select button sits on each Done day. Live tasks have no checkbox. The composer becomes a delete bar that names the folders, and you confirm with a second click.
  • A chat is a task (plan 29). It shows as 00 prep and 01 chat. The chat's inspector keeps the session levers: Continue, Verify, Handoff, Escalate and Stop.
